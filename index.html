Shooting tips
  • Lock focus and exposure. Tap and hold on the Moon (AE/AF lock), then drag exposure down until the bright limb shows detail rather than white.
  • Use your longest lens. 3×–5× optical zoom, or hold the phone to binoculars or a telescope eyepiece. Avoid digital zoom past the optical limit.
  • Shoot 20–60 frames. Burst mode or a 10-second 4K video both work. More frames means less noise.
  • Steady the phone. A tripod or a wall helps; use the timer or volume button so the tap doesn't shake it.
  • Stay at one zoom level. The stacker corrects drift but not rotation or zoom changes.
